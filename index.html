about

Andrija Mihailović

I am a Slovenia industrial designer. Throughout my studies and projects I tipped my toes into various fields of design, with the constant of maintaining social and sustainable impact in my work. My main passion still lies in conventional industrial design.

I have worked with the Academy of fine arts and design in Ljubljana, local brands and collectives, and Matters of Activity in Berlin — where I focused on design research and multidisciplinary work. I am also active in graphic design, photography and exhibition curating.
