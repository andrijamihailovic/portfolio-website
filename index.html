about

Andrija Mihailović

I'm a product designer and design researcher based in Ljubljana, currently pursuing a PhD focused on social design. I hold both a Bachelor's and Master's degree from the Academy of Fine Arts and Design in Ljubljana (ALUO), where I trained as an industrial designer.

What began as a passion for product design evolved into a broader conviction — that design, when grounded in deep research and cross-disciplinary collaboration, carries an incomparably greater purpose and impact. This belief now sits at the core of my practice.

In the early stages of my professional career, I work as an independent product designer as well as a researcher and assistant at ALUO. My expertise spans product design, design research, exhibition design, and art direction. I'm driven by a commitment to merging academic rigor with creative practice — pushing the boundaries of what design can achieve when it reaches beyond its own discipline.
